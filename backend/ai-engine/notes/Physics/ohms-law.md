# Ohm's law

Ohm's law says that the current through a conductor is directly proportional to the voltage across it, provided the temperature stays constant. In symbols, V = I × R, where V is the voltage in volts, I is the current in amperes and R is the resistance in ohms.

## Rearranged forms

Current is I = V / R and resistance is R = V / I. If the voltage doubles and the resistance stays the same, the current doubles.

## Electrical power

Power is the rate at which energy is used: P = V × I. Combining this with Ohm's law gives P = I² × R and P = V² / R, measured in watts.

## Resistors in series and parallel

In series the resistances add up: R_total = R1 + R2 + R3. In parallel the reciprocals add up: 1 / R_total = 1 / R1 + 1 / R2 + 1 / R3. A parallel combination always has a smaller resistance than its smallest branch.
